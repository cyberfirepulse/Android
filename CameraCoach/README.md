# CameraCoach

Android app for photographers in Dutch, English and Czech: camera advice (ISO, shutter speed, aperture) for your own cameras, lenses and filters (keep several cameras), a light meter, weather with rain radar, golden and blue hour calculated for your location, a shoot planner with scores per photo goal, a realistic star map (5,044 real stars, Milky Way, constellations, moon and planets, zoom and Aim mode), photo locations with compass, an Academy with 20 topics plus your own PDF/TXT material, a PDF Shoot Card and a wireless connection to Canon cameras via Canon CCAPI (for example the EOS 250D): live view, remote shutter and putting the advised settings on the camera.

By **CyberFirePulse** · contact: cyberfirepulse@gmail.com

## Download

Get the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/cyberfirepulse/Android/releases) (tags `CameraCoach-v…`).
Every release lists the SHA-256 of the APK. Check it on Windows with:

```
certutil -hashfile CameraCoach-<version>.apk SHA256
```

On the phone: open the APK and allow installing from this source when Android asks. After that the app reports new versions itself (Info → Check for updates).

## Wireless camera connection (Canon CCAPI)

Canon has to enable CCAPI once per camera. The app shows the steps (Koppeling tab): register at the Canon Developer Programme, update the camera firmware, activate CCAPI with Canon's activation tool, switch on the camera Wi-Fi and connect the phone to it.

## Data sources

Open-Meteo (weather, geocoding), RainViewer (rain radar), OpenStreetMap (map), BigDataCloud (place name), d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn (star catalogue, constellations, Milky Way; BSD licence). Sun, moon, stars and planets are calculated in the app.
